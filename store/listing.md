## Full description (max 4000)

```
Slow Vibes turns any song on your phone into slowed and reverb-soaked audio —
the warm, spacious, late-night version of a track you already love.

No editing skills needed. No settings to learn. Pick a song, tap Generate, and
Slow Vibes applies a carefully tuned Slow + Reverb preset for you.


HOW IT WORKS

1. Choose a song from your device
2. Tap Generate
3. Preview it, then download it to your Music folder

That's the whole app. Three taps from a song you have to a slowed + reverb
version you can keep.


WHAT YOU GET

• One-tap conversion — a tuned preset, no sliders to fiddle with
• Slowed tempo with the natural pitch drop that gives the style its sound
• Reverb for depth and space
• Bass warmth so the low end still carries
• Waveform preview with play, pause and seek before you commit
• Your own library of created tracks, with search
• Share any track straight to WhatsApp, Instagram, Drive or anywhere else
• Downloads saved to Music/Slow Vibes so other music players can find them
• Optional daily reminders so you remember to make something


EVERYTHING HAPPENS ON YOUR PHONE

Your music is never uploaded. All audio processing runs on your device using a
built-in audio engine, so nothing is sent to a server and nothing is stored
anywhere but your own phone. There is no sign-up, no account and no password.

The app works without an internet connection for everything except loading the
short ad shown when you download a track.


SUPPORTED FILES

Import MP3, WAV, AAC, M4A, OGG and FLAC. Exports are saved as MP3 at 192 kbps,
which plays on essentially any device or app.


A NOTE ON ADS

Slow Vibes is free. Downloading a track you have created plays one short
rewarded video ad. You choose when to watch it — nothing plays unless you tap
Download, and there are no banners or pop-ups anywhere in the app.


Made for anyone who has ever wished a song were a little slower, a little
wider, and a lot more atmospheric.

Slow it. Feel it. Vibe it.
```
