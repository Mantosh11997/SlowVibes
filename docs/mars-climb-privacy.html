Privacy Policy

Mars Climb (Android) · Last updated 18 October 2026

Mars Climb is a hill-climb racing game. This policy explains what the Android app does with your data.

Your game stays on your device. Your progress — coins, machines, upgrades, cleared courses, best distances and ghost recordings — is saved only in the app's own storage on your phone. Mars Climb has no account system and no game server, and we cannot see any of it.

Information we do not collect

Mars Climb has no accounts and no analytics. We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any contact details
  • Your game progress or how you play
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, files, messages or call history
  • Usage statistics or behavioural analytics of any kind

Information collected by advertising

Mars Climb is free and shows advertisements: an occasional full-screen ad when a run ends, and an optional video you can choose to watch to double the coins from a run. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID Serving ads, frequency capping, measuring ad performance and fraud prevention
Device information Model, operating system version, language and screen characteristics, used to select a suitable ad
IP address Approximate country-level location, used for ad relevance and legal compliance
Ad interaction data Whether an ad was shown, watched to completion, or tapped

This collection is performed by Google, not by us. We receive only aggregate, anonymous reporting — such as the total number of ads shown. We never see who you are or what you did in the game.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your device, which also lets you opt out of personalised advertising.

Your consent choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy legislation, Mars Climb asks for your choices about advertising when you first open the game, using Google's User Messaging Platform. No advertisement is requested before you have answered.

You can change your answer at any time with the privacy button (a shield icon) at the top of the game's home screen. That button appears only where these consent rules apply; elsewhere no consent form is shown and none is needed.

Network access

Mars Climb connects to the internet for one purpose only: loading advertisements. The game itself — every course, machine and sound — is part of the app and plays fully offline. If you are offline, no ads load and the game works as normal.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Mars Climb, or clearing its storage in your device settings, deletes all of your saved progress. There is nothing stored anywhere else for us to delete.

Children

Mars Climb is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from them.

Browser version

This policy covers the Android app. If you play Mars Climb in a web browser on a games website, the advertising there is provided by that website, and its own privacy policy applies.
