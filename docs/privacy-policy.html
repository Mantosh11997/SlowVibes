Privacy Policy

Slow Vibes · Last updated 31 July 2026

Slow Vibes converts audio files on your device into slowed and reverb-processed versions. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Your music never leaves your device. All audio processing happens locally using an on-device audio engine. Slow Vibes does not upload, transmit, copy or store your audio files on any server, and we cannot access them.

Information we do not collect

Slow Vibes has no account system and no analytics. We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any contact details
  • Your audio files, or any part of their contents
  • The names of the tracks you import or create
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, messages or call history
  • Usage statistics or behavioural analytics of any kind

Information collected by advertising

Slow Vibes shows a rewarded video advertisement when you choose to download a track you have created. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID Serving ads, frequency capping, measuring ad performance and fraud prevention
Device information Model, operating system version, language and screen characteristics, used to select a suitable ad
IP address Approximate country-level location, used for ad relevance and legal compliance
Ad interaction data Whether an ad was shown, viewed to completion, or tapped

This collection is performed by Google, not by us. We receive only aggregate, anonymous reporting — such as the total number of ads shown. We never see who you are or what you did in the app.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your device, which also lets you opt out of personalised advertising.

Network access

Slow Vibes connects to the internet for one purpose only: loading advertisements. Before showing an ad the app checks that a connection is available. If you are offline, no advertisement can load and the download is not completed. No other feature of the app requires or uses a network connection.

Files and storage

  • Audio you import. Files are chosen through your device's own file picker, which grants the app access to only the single file you select. Slow Vibes reads that file to process it and does not scan or index your storage.
  • Tracks you create. Saved into the app's private storage and, when you download them, into your device's public Music/Slow Vibes/ folder so they are available to other apps. They remain entirely on your device.
  • Deleting data. Deleting a track in the app removes it from the app's storage. Uninstalling Slow Vibes removes everything in its private storage; files already downloaded to your Music folder remain, and you can delete them with any file manager.

Sharing

The Share button hands the selected file to your device's system share sheet. What happens next is governed by whichever app you choose to share to. Slow Vibes does not send your files anywhere on its own.

Notifications

Slow Vibes can show two daily reminders. These are generated entirely on your device and scheduled by the Android system. No server is involved and no push notification service is used. You can turn them off at any time in your device's notification settings.

Children

Slow Vibes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no account to close and no personal information for us to export or erase. To stop all data collection associated with advertising, uninstall the app or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings as described above.
